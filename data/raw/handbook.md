# Meridian Plant Services Employee Handbook 2026.1

Meridian Plant Services is a fictional company invented for this project. Every rule and number in this handbook is made up for demonstration.

## 1. About this handbook

This handbook sets out the people policies that apply to every permanent employee. Where a manager's instruction conflicts with this handbook, the handbook applies. Questions go to the HR team through the HR assistant or the HR helpdesk.

## 2. Working hours

The standard working week is 40 hours, Sunday to Thursday, 07:00 to 15:30 with a 30-minute meal break. Shift employees follow the rota published by their unit. Overtime must be approved in advance by the line manager.

## 3. Probation

Every new employee serves a probation period of 6 months from the joining date. The manager may extend probation once, by up to 3 months, with written reasons. During probation either side may end employment with 7 days' notice.

## 4. Annual leave

### 4.1 Entitlement and accrual

Permanent employees are entitled to 21 days of annual leave per year. Leave accrues at 1.75 days for every completed month of service.

### 4.2 Requesting annual leave

Annual leave must be requested at least 7 days in advance. A single block of annual leave may not exceed 15 consecutive days without HR approval. Employees on probation may not take annual leave; accrued days become available when probation ends.

### 4.3 Carry forward

Up to 10 unused annual leave days may be carried into the next year. Days above 10 lapse on 31 December.

## 5. Sick leave

Employees have 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for any absence longer than 2 days. Sick leave cannot be carried forward.

## 6. Casual leave

Employees have 6 days of casual leave per year for short personal matters. Casual leave may not be taken for more than 2 consecutive days and may not be combined with annual leave.

## 7. Payroll

Salaries are paid on the last working day of each month. Any change that affects pay, such as overtime, allowances or unpaid leave, must reach payroll by the 20th of the month to be included in that month's salary. Changes received after the 20th are paid in the following month.

## 8. Travel and expenses

### 8.1 Daily allowance

Employees on approved business travel receive a daily allowance by grade. Domestic: grade 1 USD 60, grade 2 USD 80, grade 3 USD 110, grade 4 USD 150. International: grade 1 USD 120, grade 2 USD 160, grade 3 USD 220, grade 4 USD 300.

### 8.2 Reimbursement claims

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of returning from travel, with receipts. A claim is only valid for travel that was approved before it took place. Claims above USD 2,500 need a director's approval in addition to the line manager's.

## 9. Remote and hybrid work

Roles are classed as office, hybrid or remote. Hybrid employees receive a monthly remote work allowance of USD 150 and remote employees USD 300, to cover internet and home office costs. Office-based roles receive no allowance.

## 10. Resignation and notice

An employee who resigns must give written notice. The notice period depends on grade: grades 1 and 2 give 30 days, grade 3 gives 60 days and grade 4 gives 90 days. Notice runs from the day after HR receives the letter. Annual leave may not be used to shorten the notice period without the manager's agreement.

### 10.1 Exit checklist

Before the last working day the employee returns company equipment, hands over work and documents to the manager, completes the exit interview and clears any advances. Final settlement is paid with the next payroll after clearance.

## 11. Code of conduct

Employees treat colleagues with respect, follow every safety rule and permit-to-work procedure, and protect confidential information. Harassment, discrimination and retaliation are not tolerated and can be reported to HR in confidence.

## 12. Data privacy

HR keeps personal data only for employment purposes. Employees may ask HR for a copy of their own records. HR never shares an employee's salary, health or personal details with other employees.
